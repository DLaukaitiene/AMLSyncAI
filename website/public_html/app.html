What it does

Everything AMLR asks of you, without the spreadsheets.

The same standards our analysts apply for regulated financial companies, built into software for firms that need to run compliance themselves.

Client onboarding portal

Send one secure link. Your client fills in the questionnaire, uploads documents and confirms ownership — on their phone, in minutes.

Identity checks

National eID or ID document with chip read, liveness and face match. Images, video and the report are saved to the client file.

Sanctions, PEP and adverse media

EU and UN sanctions lists included and re-checked whenever they change; ownership chains are added up for the 50% rule.

Beneficial owners, calculated

Enter the structure once. AMLSync App finds everyone who owns or controls 25% or more and screens each of them.

Risk assessment and hard gates

Every client gets a risk score with reasons. A client can't be accepted, and a matter can't complete, while a required step is missing.

Compliance calendar

Refresh dates, reports and deadlines in one place, with automatic reminders to your clients and your team.

Firm risk assessment and policies

Your business-wide risk assessment, AML policies and procedures drafted from your answers — reviewed and approved by you.

Suspicion reports

Record internal suspicions, decide and prepare reports to your FIU, with the full audit trail kept.

Ready for the supervisor

A complete, exportable file per client and for your firm: identity, ownership, screening, risk, decisions and training records.

How it works

From sign-up to your first compliant client in a day.

Set up your firm

Choose your country and sector. The app drafts your risk assessment and policies for your review.

Invite your client

The client completes the questionnaire, uploads documents and verifies their identity through a secure link.

Review and decide

Screening, ownership and risk are worked out for you. You approve, and every step is recorded.

Stay up to date

Automatic re-screening, refresh reminders and deadlines keep each file current.
