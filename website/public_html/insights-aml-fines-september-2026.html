Three AML fines in September 2026 – and the basic failings behind them

2 October 2026 · Deimena Laukaitienė, Founder

Three AML enforcement actions from September 2026:

  • Julius Baer – CHF 10 million (FINMA)
  • Banque SBA – €750,000 (Central Bank of Cyprus)
  • Volksbank Düsseldorf Neuss – €210,000 (BaFin)

The failings are basic ones

Where the details are public, the problems are not exotic: due diligence on high-risk clients and ongoing monitoring.

The European Banking Authority sees the same picture across the EU: 61% of AML breaches come from customer due diligence.

Procedures exist – capacity doesn't

In most cases the procedures are in place. What is missing is the time and people to run them every day: periodic reviews slip, EDD files are left incomplete, monitoring alerts age in the queue.

With the AMLR applying from July 2027 – bringing fixed CDD refresh cycles and more prescriptive requirements – that gap will only get more expensive.

Where teams usually fall behind first

  • Onboarding – pressure to approve quickly, incomplete documentation.
  • Periodic reviews – the first thing to slip when the team is stretched.
  • Alerts – growing backlogs and ageing cases.
