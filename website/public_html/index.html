Security & control

Your data. Your processes. Your decisions.

Our own set-up has to meet the standard we help you keep. Our analysts support your team, your accountability and your goals.

  • We work inside your systems. Wherever possible, analysts use your tools with role-based access you control — no copies of client files.
  • Contracts before access. Confidentiality terms and a GDPR data processing agreement are signed before anyone starts.
  • Your procedures, followed. We work to your policies, risk appetite and escalation rules, with decisions kept with your responsible officers.
  • Everything documented. Case notes, progress reports and a written handover, so your audit trail stays complete.
