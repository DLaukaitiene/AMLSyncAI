AMLA central AML/CFT database: supervisory findings will travel across the EU

15 October 2026 · Deimena Laukaitienė, Founder

On 7 October 2026 the EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA) opened a consultation on its central AML/CFT database, with a public hearing on 3 November.

What the database will contain

Supervisors across the EU will report into one shared system. For banks, EMIs and CASPs this includes:

  • sanctions and other supervisory measures,
  • licence withdrawals,
  • fit and proper assessments,
  • identified ML/TF risks.

Why it matters for Lithuanian firms

If you are a Lithuanian firm passporting into other member states, a Bank of Lithuania finding will no longer stay at home. Supervisors in other countries will be able to see it – and take it into account in their own work.

AMLA expects no new reporting costs for firms: supervisors do the reporting. The work for firms is different – closing findings properly and keeping the evidence to show it.

Closing findings, not just acknowledging them

Most compliance teams know exactly what needs fixing after an inspection or audit. What is usually missing is the hours to do it while daily onboarding, reviews and alerts keep coming in. A practical approach:

  1. Keep one findings tracker with owners, deadlines and evidence requirements.
  2. Split remediation into defined sprints rather than squeezing it between daily tasks.
  3. Store the evidence of closure alongside each finding – that is what a supervisor will ask for.
