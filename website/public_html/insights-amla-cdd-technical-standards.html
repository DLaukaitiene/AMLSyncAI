AMLA finalises three AMLR technical standards – start your CDD gap check now

5 October 2026 · Deimena Laukaitienė, Founder

On 1 October 2026, AMLA finalised three technical standards under the AMLR and sent them to the European Commission for adoption.

The three standards

  1. Customer due diligence (CDD) – the one most teams will feel. It spells out what you need to collect and verify, including PEP screening that also covers family members and close associates.
  2. Linked transactions – how to identify transactions that should be treated together.
  3. Group-wide controls – AML/CFT policies and controls across groups.

The timing

  • The standards apply 6 months after they enter into force.
  • The AMLR itself applies from 10 July 2027.

Why the work starts now

July 2027 sounds far off, but the practical work is already here. Take your current onboarding forms and a sample of existing client files and check them against the new requirements:

  • Do you collect every data point the CDD standard requires?
  • Does your PEP screening cover family members and close associates?
  • How many existing files would need to be refreshed?

The gaps usually turn up faster than you would expect – and the earlier you find them, the more time you have to fix them without disrupting onboarding.
